Quantitative results on separate matched cohorts

TabletopGen completed 59 of 80 attempted scenes; PAT3D completed 67 of 80, with 13 failures and no jobs remaining. Each baseline is compared with SceneRig on its own completed scenes, so SceneRig has two separate rows. Failed runs are excluded, not zero-filled; no completed scenes were excluded based on appearance.

All applicable completed scenes contribute to these averages; the four examples above are qualitative illustrations. Object and layout scores use the synthetic subsets: 38 scenes for TabletopGen and 35 for PAT3D.

Reproduction and evaluation details

PAT3D starts from the supplied benchmark image and uses Hunyuan3D 2.1, GPT-Image-2, and Claude Opus 5. Its original layout, mesh processing, and physics pipeline are retained with the approved segmentation-mask loading fix. Reproduction encountered API rejections, asset/physics failures, and camera-export failures; the completion counts describe this reproduction, not the authors’ reported success rate.

PAT3D’s source camera uses DepthPro intrinsics and the estimated global gravity/support alignment, then follows only the reference support’s vertical displacement during physics. It does not follow individual object pose edits, and the support is not frozen. This is approximate source-camera recovery, with no ground-truth camera fitting or image-score optimization.

PAT3D source scores and panels use the saved Cycles 32-sample render. TabletopGen source scores use its saved pyrender output, whereas its qualitative panels use archived Cycles 96-sample VLM renders. SceneRig uses its existing Cycles evaluation images. Renderer versions, samples, lighting and backgrounds are not normalized across methods. TabletopGen uses supplied synthetic calibration and estimated real-image intrinsics; PAT3D estimates both from its reconstruction.

Nearby views apply the same +30° orbit rule while preserving each method’s orbital radius and elevation; they do not imply identical extrinsics. Images are resized and WebP-encoded for display, with no brightness adjustment. Metrics use the original images.

TabletopGen’s 21 incomplete runs comprise 17 top-view quality-check failures, three Seedream input rejections, and one missing support surface. Additional TabletopGen calibrated/novel-view metrics remain available; those extra pixel metrics are outside the PAT3D comparison reported here.
