Rebuttal videos for Reviewer Xv61

Each example shows a real-world policy rollout and rollouts of the same policy in the SceneRig (ours) and SimFoundry reconstructions of that episode’s initial scene. Real and simulated rollouts run independently, so their timing differs.

Question 1: Is the SimFoundry mustard failure a coincidence?

“The video ‘Put mustard bottle in the left bin’ of SimFoundry looks like a coincidence: the left robot arm accidentally pushed the left bin, which fell over and caused the task to fail. I would like to see another video of this.”

Reviewer Xv61

Below are two more Mustard-to-bin episodes. In both, SceneRig’s outcome matches the real outcome and SimFoundry’s does not.

π0.5, episode 9

The real robot and SceneRig both place the mustard in the left bin. In SimFoundry, the left bin tips over again and the mustard comes to rest on top of it.

LingBot-VLA 2.0, episode 7

The real arm pushes the left bin aside and the mustard is not placed. SceneRig reproduces this failure, while SimFoundry reports a success.

Question 2: Why does a simulator succeed when the real robot fails?

“I would like to see one sample of where SimFoundry/SceneRig succeeded but did not meet the agreement, and some analysis of why they failed.”

Reviewer Xv61

Below are two episodes in which a simulator reports success but the real robot fails.

LingBot-VLA 2.0, Everything-to-bin, episode 3

The pear starts near the far edge of the robot’s reach. In the real rollout, the gripper pushes it farther away, out of reach; SceneRig reproduces this, and both fail. SimFoundry reconstructs the pear smaller, which makes it easier to grasp, so its rollout succeeds. The simulated success comes from a reconstruction error that SceneRig avoids.

π0.5, Fruit-to-plate, episode 0

The real robot places the banana and the pear but leaves the apple beside the plate, while both simulations succeed. A possible reason is appearance: in the real image, the red apple and the red plate look similar, which may make it hard for the policy to notice that the apple is still outside the plate. Both simulated renderings show slightly higher contrast between them.
