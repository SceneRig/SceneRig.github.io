Mustard-to-bin: two more episodes

Two further Mustard-to-bin episodes. In both, SceneRig’s outcome matches the real outcome and SimFoundry’s does not.

π0.5 · Mustard-to-bin · episode 9“Put the mustard into the left bin.”

The real robot and SceneRig both place the mustard in the left bin. In SimFoundry, the left bin tips over and the mustard comes to rest on top of it.

LingBot-VLA 2.0 · Mustard-to-bin · episode 7“Put the mustard into the left bin.”

Here the real arm also pushes the left bin aside, and the mustard is not placed. SceneRig reproduces this failure, while SimFoundry reports a success.

Simulated success, real failure

Two episodes in which a simulator reports success but the real robot fails, with our analysis.

LingBot-VLA 2.0 · Everything-to-bin · episode 3“Put everything into the bin.”

Analysis. The pear starts near the far edge of the robot’s reach. In the real rollout, the gripper pushes it farther away, out of reach; SceneRig reproduces this, and both fail. SimFoundry reconstructs the pear smaller, which makes it easier to grasp, so its rollout succeeds. The simulated success here comes from a reconstruction error that SceneRig avoids.

π0.5 · Fruit-to-plate · episode 0“Put all fruits in the red plate.”

Analysis. The real robot places the banana and the pear but leaves the apple beside the plate, while both simulations succeed. A possible reason is appearance: in the real image, the red apple and the red plate look similar, which may make it hard for the policy to notice that the apple is still outside the plate. Both simulated renderings show slightly higher contrast between them.
